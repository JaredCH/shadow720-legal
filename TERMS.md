## What Shadow720 is

Shadow720 is a peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted group app. It lets a group of
people share their location, chat, and files with each other.

There is no account and no server operated by the developer. The app talks directly
to public Nostr relays and Blossom file servers, which store only encrypted data.

## Your responsibilities

- **You control your group.** Whoever creates a group chooses the relays and file
  servers it uses, and decides who receives an invite.
- **Guard your invite.** An invite string plus the join password is what lets someone
  join your group and read everything the group key protects. Share it only with
  people you trust, over a channel you trust.
- **Guard your device.** Your keys live in a vault protected by the Android Keystore.
  If the device is reset or its hardware key is invalidated, the vault becomes
  unreadable and you will need a fresh invite.
- **Do not use the app to break the law.** You are responsible for what you share and
  for complying with the law where you are.

## Location sharing

Location sharing is opt-in and requires your explicit permission, including the
"Allow all the time" grant for background sharing. While background sharing is
active, a persistent notification is shown and can be stopped at any time.

You are responsible for deciding whether sharing your location with a particular
group is appropriate. Do not use the app to track anyone without their knowledge and
consent.
