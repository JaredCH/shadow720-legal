# Shadow720 legal

Public legal documents for the Shadow720 Android app.

- [Privacy policy](./PRIVACY.md)
- [Terms of use](./TERMS.md)

These are the canonical versions referenced from the app and from the Google Play
listing. The app links to the privacy policy from its settings screen and from the
first-run consent gate.

## Why this repository exists

Google Play requires a publicly reachable privacy policy URL for any app that
requests sensitive permissions, and Shadow720 requests background location. Hosting
the policy here keeps it versioned alongside the app it describes, and keeps the URL
stable across releases.
